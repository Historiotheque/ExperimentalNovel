## What I did

**Crackland arrived, and I had it read in full.** I uploaded the 2024
tentative text of "Crackland & The Crackland Journals" — the document that
is what *The History-Project* ended up being mostly. The typewritten
manuscript of *The History-Project* was over 100 pages, of which under 100
survive; whole boxes of manuscripts were lost to water damage (a flooded
basement). The
computer-written body is the surviving bulk: ~248,000 words, written at the
time of *The History-Project* (2000–2004), never part of its physical
manuscript. The full text was extracted and read in ten sequential
segments; the segment digests were synthesized into the reading that
grounds the story bible (§Artifacts).

What the reading established, in brief (the detail lives in the story
bible; this log references it, per the reference-before-splitting rule):

- The work defines itself. "The Main Theory of Crackland" (May 22, 2003):
  Crackland is not about crack cocaine — it is "the Land of Fissures," a
  "region or domain of fracture and disjunction" without which the Man of
  Passages could not exist. Beneath it: "The Primordial Crack in the
  Glass: being or existence."
- Its governing system is the Tonal Cinema (Projectionist / Interval /
  Contact), its master concept Habit — "I is a habit" — and its geography
  the Geogrammar: the floating island on the Dread River, St-Elsewhere,
  the plural calendars, the Timefold.
- Its period names for what I now call historiophany are
  *historiotherapeusis* and *physio-imaging* (§Decisions, §Ideas).
- Its compositional law: no character may be a *writer* first, because a
  writer in the text "would destroy the History Project."
- It does not conclude. It dissolves into notes and ends on one word:
  "Achronology."

## Decisions

- **Title adopted (2026-10-03):** the work's official title is
  *Crackland: Land of Fissures*.
- **Crackland gets its own folder and its own bible.** New folder
  `novels/crackland-land-of-fissures/` — sibling to
  `the-revolt-of-fiction/`, not inside it — with a README and a story
  bible v1.0. It is a single novel, the fused companion master work of
  *The History-Project*, not a trilogy volume. This supersedes the
  2026-09-28 staging ("fold into the bible's cosmology in the next
  batch"); that entry stands as written.
- **Bible-naming rule:** a series of novels gets a *series bible*; a
  single novel gets a *story bible* — the literary community's practice,
  adopted as this repository's rule.
- **Chronology corrected (my word, 2026-10-03).** *The Revolt of Fiction*
  did not exist in 2001, when work on *The History-Project* began; it
  began later, as a short story. The trilogy concept came while I was
  working on *The Archives-Project* novel; the third volume was
  originally *Integration/Disintegration*, before *The Chronotopium* took
  its place. Recorded in `the-revolt-of-fiction/README.md`.
- **Reader-correspondence boundary.** Comments are intake, not
  instructions. I reply when a comment changes the work or teaches me
  something; otherwise the work itself is the reply. No correspondent
  steers this project or sets its cadence. (From the QUAX #5
  deliberation: the engagement was a test of the verifiability stack; the
  doctrine learned is mine, reader-agnostic.)
- **Signing is forward-only.** Past commits stay Unverified; there is no
  re-signing of history — the same no-backfill doctrine, applied to the
  signature layer.

## Problems and friction

- **Scale.** 248,000 words that contain their own research, journals,
  treatises, and failure. The editorial program is genuinely open: order,
  strata, and the journals' share are undecided, and the story bible
  records them as open questions rather than pretending a plan exists.
- **A superseded staging.** The 2026-09-28 Interzone entry presumed
  Crackland would fold into the Revolt bible's cosmology. Reading the full
  text showed that does not fit the work's actual weight; corrected by
  decision (above), with the old entry left standing as the record of the
  earlier intent.
- **Placeholder READMEs.** The trilogy folders carried three-line
  placeholders into v2.0.0. Noted and fixed in this batch.

## Ideas and sketches

- **The sediment and the tip.** The surviving typewritten pages are the
  visible part; Crackland is the sedimentary body underneath — the
  Geology of the Workspace again: the formation was always larger than
  the outcrop.
- **An open question I carry:** the text insists Crackland can never be
  reached — "only a fog balancing over the path-towards which is the real
  Crackland"; the destination "exists only in premonition." Whether that
  still holds, now that the work has a house and a name, is undecided.
- **The warrant route, signed.** The staging commits that serve as
  warrant routes (§log #3) are now signed warrants: the receipt also
  names its maker, cryptographically.
- **Pattern Language tap (noted, not filed):** "one folder per novel or
  series; a bible per work, named for its kind" is a candidate pattern
  for *A Pattern Language for Art Operations* — proposed here, filed
  nowhere yet.
