## Decisions

- **Decision:** The trilogy is **The Revolt of Fiction**; vol. 1 *The
  History-Project*; vol. 2 *The Archives-Project*; vol. 3 *The Chronotopium:
  A Story of Algorithmogenesis*. **Reason:** My emphatic words — "try to never
  get that wrong." The trilogy is named after the EVENT: the fiction revolts
  (characters put the Author on trial; the created turns on the creator).
- **Decision:** The Author (capital A) is the fictional author inside the
  novels, distinct from me the writer. **Reason:** The trial needs a defendant.
  (I liked the Author/writer distinction.)
- **Decision:** Chronos (C-H-R-O-N-O-S), personification of time — never Kronos
  the Titan. **Reason:** My correction.
- **Decision:** Trilogy form = disintegration gradient — vol. 1 starts
  traditional and disintegrates as the in-novel Archives-Project autopsies it;
  vol. 2 = fragments/remnants; vol. 3 = philosophical software (real Python,
  the Archivillus system). **Reason:** Vol. 1 failed for lack of a workspace
  theory; vol. 2 failed by studying failure (failure by design — the
  post-mortem team catches the disease it autopsies); vol. 3 = Archivillus
  becomes executable.
- **Decision:** New ideas stage in the Interzone as dated entries; the bible
  changes only in batches; folded entries are marked, never silently rewritten.
  **Reason:** No backfilling — the audit trail is the credibility.
- **Decision:** Format/tool decisions are LOCAL, never global. **Reason:** The
  MyST incident — a propagating convention is how corruption spreads, the
  opposite of the incorruptibility doctrine.
- **Decision:** References are an *illuminated bibliography*: Chicago citation
  + my interpretation. The doctrine is **winks, not theft** — interpreted
  properly, attributed fully, never imitated. **Reason:** Maximal originality;
  Acker is the contrast case.
- **Decision:** The repo gets one folder per novel (`novels/` proposed;
  `projects/` the open alternative), following the repo-structure spec's
  project template; REFMATS.md plays the spec's BIBLIOGRAPHY.md role.
  **Reason:** My words win over the spec's labels; stick to the specs otherwise.
- **Decision:** This log is the first novel log; novel logs continue per
  session, in the first person. **Reason:** My instruction — everything on
  record before it's lost; and the log voice rule (below).
- **Decision:** No personal material in the experimental-novel repository —
  the novels are not about me. My personal writings (Hidden Stirrings / An
  Unseen Landscape, Solitude and Death, the Happiness and Death essays, the
  Book About Everything) live outside the repos and off GitHub, private and
  crypto-Kierkegaardian. **Reason:** The repo is the work's public record;
  the journals are the life's private one.
