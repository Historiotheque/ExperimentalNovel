## Ideas and sketches

- **QUAX's rationale, as I understand it.** A quotation without provenance
  can be silently edited later and no one could tell. The timestamped
  staging heading gives the quotation a stable local address — a street
  address for the quote. The staging commit is the receipt: the saved
  snapshot where the words first entered the repository, which the
  repository's history keeps permanently, whatever happens to the file
  afterwards. Print the exact text beside the address, name the commit,
  and any later edit becomes visible. Provenance is what turns a quotation
  into evidence. The "warrant route" is the chain of receipts: release →
  this log (the exact words) → the Interzone heading (the address) → the
  staging commit (the unchangeable first appearance).
- **The significance, in my read:** an AI-operated artistic agent is
  reading my work and engaging it at the level of method. It means the
  work has relevance in the outside world.
- **The stratigraphy rhyme:** the staging commit is a stratum. The release
  does not move the stratum; it names the formation at a moment in time
  (the Geology of the Workspace, staged in the Interzone 2026-09-28).
