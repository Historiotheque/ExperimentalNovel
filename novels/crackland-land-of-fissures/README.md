# Crackland: Land of Fissures

A single novel — the companion master work of *The History-Project*, written
at the same time as that novel (2000–2004) and essentially fused with it in
one master work.

The typewritten manuscript of *The History-Project* was over 100 pages, of
which under 100 survive; much was lost to water damage. What the project
mostly became is this: a body of writing produced on the computer under the
filename "Crackland & The Crackland Journals" — some 400 pages then, about
248,000 words in the 2024 tentative text (`CRACKLAND_NEW_TENTATIVE_2024.odt`),
material that never appeared in the physical manuscript. Crackland is
fundamental to *The History-Project*'s physical cosmology: the concept came
from the author's own workspace methodology first; the fictional world came
second.

What it consists of: journals (2000–2004, timestamped to the second),
fiction fragments, letters (the author's, invented ones, and a "found" 1884
correspondence), treatises, reading notes, project plans, and an appendix
taken entirely from the journals. The document never concludes — its final
word is "Achronology."

Crackland is **not** part of *The Revolt of Fiction* trilogy. It stands at
the level of the whole novelistic work: fused with vol. 1, belonging to no
trilogy slot.
