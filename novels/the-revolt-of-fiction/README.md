# The Revolt of Fiction

The trilogy — named after the event: the fiction revolts. The characters put
the Author on trial; the created turns on the creator.

Three novels:

1. *The History-Project*
2. *The Archives-Project*
3. *The Chronotopium: A Story of Algorithmogenesis*

Working order: begin with *The Archives-Project*.

Genesis: the series began as a short story, later than the start of *The
History-Project* (2001). The trilogy was conceived while *The
Archives-Project* was being written; the third volume's slot was originally
held by *Integration/Disintegration* before *The Chronotopium* took its
place.

Companion work, outside the trilogy: *Crackland: Land of Fissures*
(`../crackland-land-of-fissures/`) — a single novel fused with *The
History-Project*, governed by its own story bible.

A work-level system (DC-2026-006: The Revolt of Fiction System) — the novels,
the process of writing them, and the design concepts, as one governed unit.
Governance: the series bible (`revolt-of-fiction_series-bible_2026-09-26.md`)
— canonical text, changed only in batches folded from the Interzone. Per-novel
design concepts to come, one per novel folder. More series bibles will come,
one per series.

Status: scaffolding — folders and manuscripts to come.
