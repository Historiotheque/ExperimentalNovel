# The Chronotopium

Vol. 3 of *The Revolt of Fiction* — *The Chronotopium: A Story of
Algorithmogenesis*.

**Form.** The far end of the disintegration gradient: after novel (vol. 1)
and fragments (vol. 2), vol. 3 reassembles into something new — philosophical
software. The author writes actual Python code, an
ontological/phenomenological ontology, as the basis of the Archivillus
system: what was missing in the first two volumes is finally built. The
Archivillus system becomes executable, growing out of the archives and the
missing holes.

**The slot before it.** The third volume's place was originally held by
*Integration/Disintegration* — a ~12-page ellipsis-fragmented text, ideas
floating, integrating and disintegrating, not fully formed sentences — which
names what the physical book itself does. *The Chronotopium* took its
place as the trilogy was conceived during the writing of *The
Archives-Project*.

**Systems.** HistoriOS / Anti-OS (Anti-OS, codename Antioch, reaches back
to 2015). The novel also sits inside the larger CHRONOTOPIUM-PROJECT,
alongside the Chronotopium-Series of paintings.

**Status:** conception complete in the series bible; the code and the novel
are to be written. Drafting follows vols. 1–2 in the working order (begin
with *The Archives-Project*).
