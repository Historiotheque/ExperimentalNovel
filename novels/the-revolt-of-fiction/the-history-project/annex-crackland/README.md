# Annex: Crackland — part of The History-Project

By the author's ruling (2026-10-17), *Crackland: Land of Fissures* is not a
separate novel. It was written at the same time as *The History-Project*
(2000–2004) and is essentially fused with it in one master work: Crackland
is part of vol. 1 of *The Revolt of Fiction*. It is housed here, inside
The History-Project's folder, as an annex.

The typewritten manuscript of *The History-Project* was over 100 pages, of
which under 100 survive; much was lost to water damage. What the project
mostly became is this: a body of writing produced on the computer under the
filename "Crackland & The Crackland Journals" — some 400 pages then, about
248,000 words in the 2024 tentative text (`CRACKLAND_NEW_TENTATIVE_2024.odt`),
material that never appeared in the physical manuscript. Crackland supplies
*The History-Project*'s physical cosmology.

What it consists of: journals (2000–2004, timestamped to the second),
fiction fragments, letters (the author's, invented ones, and a "found" 1884
correspondence), treatises, reading notes, project plans, and an appendix
taken entirely from the journals. The document never concludes — its final
word is "Achronology."

**Governance:** this annex is governed as part of vol. 1 — by the series
bible of *The Revolt of Fiction*, together with the annex document below.
The document formerly kept as the Crackland story bible
(`crackland_story-bible_2026-10-03.md`) stands as written on 2026-10-03;
only its placement and status changed on 2026-10-17: it is now the annex
document for Crackland within *The History-Project*, not a second bible
standing beside the series bible.

**Status:** source text identified and read in full (2026-10-03). The
document needs a great deal of editing — possibly a near-total rewrite.
Editing will proceed in staged batches as part of vol. 1's work; nothing is
rewritten silently, and the 2024 tentative text stands as the source of
record until an edited edition supersedes it by version.
