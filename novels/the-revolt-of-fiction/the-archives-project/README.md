# The Archives-Project

Vol. 2 of *The Revolt of Fiction* — and the volume the trilogy is begun
with: the dearest volume, its materials the most developed.

**Form.** What vol. 1's autopsy leaves behind: fragments, remnants, the
archive's remains. Inside the fiction, this project failed because it was
studying failure — "when you create a theory of failure, you become
impregnated by the failure you're studying." The post-mortem team catches
the disease it is autopsying: failure by design.

**Genesis of the trilogy.** The trilogy concept itself was conceived while
this novel was being worked on — *The Revolt of Fiction* as a named series
began later than *The History-Project* (2001), first as a short story. The
third volume's slot was originally held by *Integration/Disintegration*
before *The Chronotopium* took its place.

**The material.** The 2015–2016 typewritten manuscript, transcribed (the
GitBook transcription, 2021); the fragments file of 2025, where the
Archivillus figure walks; the new-material file, culminating in the
HistoriOS System; the manuscript narrative; and the machine overviews and
historiophany reframing of 2025. Notes reach back to 2015 (the
Historiomics materials, Construction-Land, Archives-Land).

**Status:** the most developed body of material in the trilogy. Start here.
